Automatic roadside placement

Buildings, lots, and scenery snap beside nearby curved roads—not on the lanes. Frontages follow the local road direction, and road shoulders stay clear.

Natural scenery stays in forests when far from roads. Garage, dealer, and fuel markers stay off-road; race starts and checkpoints remain on the roadway.

Placement protection is always on for new or moved objects.

Scenery painting

Choose Scenery (P), enable hold-and-drag, then hold the left mouse button to scatter continuously—even without moving. One stroke is one undo step.
