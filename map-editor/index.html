WORLD TOOLS

Road designer

5 m

Select an existing road to add or extend its control points from the Inspector. Shift-click the selected road to insert a point immediately.

Terrain & biomes

Scatter trees, palms, rocks, or bushes into a selected land region. Roads stay clear.

Expand Game Map

Increase the playable boundary without resizing or moving anything already on the map. New space appears east (+X) as ocean and south (+Y) as natural land.

No existing roads, buildings, objects or terrain will be moved. Undo reverses uncommitted expansion.

World details

Open Game Map to use Streetbound's real roof, foliage and rock atlas. Parked traffic also renders from the game's vehicle textures.

With Select active, click or drag a visible house, tree, rock or ordinary building to make it editable automatically. Special gameplay buildings remain protected. Ctrl-click is also available.

Game art is referenced directly from the project, not embedded into JSON. External PNG/WebP import remains for standalone maps.

Manual placement & optional road alignment

Place and drag scenery exactly where you click. Road edits never move nearby buildings. Overlapping road footprints are shown in Map health, not silently repaired.

Choose Align selected to road to move one object deliberately, or align all overlapping scenery with confirmation. Gameplay markers always stay at their chosen positions.

Manual positions are preserved. Automatic alignment only runs when requested.

One-click environment presets

Create coherent streetscapes, real paved parking access, landscape a selected terrain region, or furnish industry. Every preset is one Undo step.

Parking access connects to the street. Stalls and parked traffic-model cars are generated at world bake; landscaping stays clear of driving aisles.

Select a road or terrain region for targeted presets. Parking uses the viewport center.

Scenery painting

Choose Scenery (P), enable hold-and-drag, then hold the left mouse button to scatter continuously—even without moving. One stroke is one undo step.

Open Game Map loads the actual baked roads, biomes, destinations and procedural scenery. Edit road handles or place scenery, then Apply to Game to rebuild the playable world. Restart a running game to see the updates.
