Streetbound Aston Martin overhead car body sprites

DB9, DB11, DB12, DBS, Vantage, Vanquish, Rapide, DBX, Valkyrie, One-77.
Each file: 887 x 1774 pixels, RGBA PNG; rear at top, nose at bottom; no drawn wheels or tires.

Detail level: The first six were generated as separate high-resolution images. Rapide, DBX, Valkyrie and One-77 were cropped from a 10-car generated artwork sheet, so they have lower native detail (upscaled to the standard canvas). These four are useful as prototype sprites but may need standalone higher-detail replacements.

The sprites do not include wheel pivot positions or calibrated real-world dimensions.
