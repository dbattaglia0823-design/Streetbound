# Morrow Coast scenery atlas

Asset: `assets/scenery/morrow-atlas-v1.png` (1774 × 887 RGBA), generated for this project using the built-in image generation tool. The original atlas is stored unchanged in the workspace. `scripts/world/coast_art.gd` selects its eight UV regions; `scenery_cutout.gdshader` suppresses the very faint translucent matte at rendering time. The scene still owns metre-scaled footprints and collision shapes independently of the art. Atlas mipmaps are enabled. Roads, terrain, parking, props outside these eight categories and the atlas UI remain procedural/vector artwork.

Layout: house, coastal cottage, cafe, workshop; oak, pine, palm, rocks. No third-party game's art is used.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production game scenery sprite atlas PNG for Streetbound, a cozy illustrated top-down driving world. Create exactly EIGHT isolated sprites in a strict 4 column by 2 row evenly spaced grid, on a truly transparent background. Each sprite stays entirely inside its own equal-size grid cell, centered, with at least 8 percent transparent padding. Row 1 left to right: (1) a cozy suburban rectangular house roof, muted terracotta roof tiles, cream gutters, tiny chimney and skylight; (2) a coastal cottage rectangular roof, sage green standing-seam roof, pale warm trim and a small dormer; (3) a small neighborhood cafe roof, warm coral tiles, cream trim, striped sage awning, small roof vent; (4) an automotive workshop rectangular flat roof, warm off-white parapet, several teal skylights and detailed rooftop air conditioning. Row 2 left to right: (5) mature oak canopy with layered rounded leaf clusters in soft moss and sage greens; (6) dense mature pine canopy seen exactly from straight overhead, asymmetric radial layers of forest-green needles; (7) overhead palm with feathery curved sage fronds; (8) a natural cluster of rounded pale warm-gray boulders with subtle fissures. All subjects seen EXACTLY VERTICALLY FROM ABOVE, a 90-degree orthographic overhead camera: ROOFS and treetops only, no front-facing facades, no perspective or isometric view. A premium hand-painted game illustration with fine material details, gentle shading inside shapes, soft edges, quiet pleasant greens, warm sand and terracotta accents. Readable at driving-game sprite scale. No harsh shadows, no cast shadows outside the subjects, no ground, no grass patch behind subjects, no roads, no background, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no borders, no text or logos. Each subject must be a separate cutout with real transparent alpha, never touching another cell. House and cafe footprints approximately rectangular with their long axis vertical in the atlas; trees approximately round. These assets will replace simple colored vector scenery in a coherent illustrated racing world; maintain consistent diffuse midday light and material rendering across all eight sprites.


## Mountain relief artwork

`mountain-relief-data.png` and `mountain-relief-map.png` are original procedural bakes from `tools/mountain_field.py` / `tools/bake_mountain_relief.py`, not downloaded terrain, AI artwork or extracted game assets. The former stores northwest sunlight, normalized visual height, slope and coverage in RGBA; it is sampled as data (no sRGB conversion). The latter is colored hillshade for the in-game atlas. Both cover the northwest 2,800 × 2,400 m in 1,400 × 1,200 pixels and use mipmaps. Texture creation requires NumPy and Pillow; gameplay uses the shipped PNGs. Rock cuts and fill slopes are baked geometry with procedural stone shading. Shadows project the existing atlas silhouettes consistently toward the southeast. Research and links are recorded in `docs/MORROW_COAST.md`.
