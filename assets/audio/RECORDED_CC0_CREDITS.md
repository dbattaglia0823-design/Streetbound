# Imported recorded audio: source and rights

## Asphalt tire squeal

- In-game asset: `tire_screech_recorded_cc0.wav`
- Original recording: **Chrysler LHS tire squeal 02 (04-25-2009).wav**
- Author: **audible-edge**
- Freesound URL: https://freesound.org/people/audible-edge/sounds/71737/
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, verified on the Freesound sound page
- Recorded vehicle: 1996 Chrysler LHS, 3.5L V6
- Recorded with Rode NTG2 and Zoom H4
- Imported edited file source: https://github.com/egore/openstreetmap-racer/blob/main/sounds/tire_screech.wav
- Processing as documented by the mirror: the first tire squeal was cropped
  to ~0.73 s, mixed down to mono and volume-adjusted; small fade edges avoid
  loop clicks. See upstream `sounds/SOURCES.md`.
- Original source's license remains CC0. Credit is optional but recommended.

This clip replaces the previous synthetic default for **asphalt wheel slip
only**. The sound is controlled by tire slip and wheelspin, not by the
equipped engine model. Dirt/gravel tire playback remains unchanged.

## Generic engine recordings

See `engines/generic_cc0/README.md`. Six CC0 pitch variants of **one**
public-domain-derived car-engine recording are blended in a quiet recorded
texture layer only when a combustion car lacks a dedicated recording.
