# Phase 8 — Living world

Single-player worlds have a per-world clock starting at noon. One in-game day takes 40 real minutes of unpaused gameplay. The clock persists separately from career saves, and Test Drive uses a temporary clock. Multiplayer and local split screen retain existing shared behavior. The driving HUD shows day, time, local weather and current road event; daylight transitions smoothly.

Park at Laurel garage, the designated nearby Laurel residence, or an enabled ground-level authored Home/Garage marker. Click **REST / SKIP TIME** to wait 1, 3, 6 or 12 hours or rest until 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 or midnight. Time skipping is blocked when moving, during races/jobs/police heat, or in multiplayer. It grants no repairs, fuel, rewards or real-time cooldown changes. Custom maps can author Home markers using the native or browser marker editor.

Rush hours 07:00–09:30 and 16:00–19:00 increase ambient vehicle budget by 50%; overnight density decreases. Race caps retain their existing behavior. Dealerships open 08:00–20:00; paid station repairs 07:00–19:00; fuel and personal garages remain available around the clock. Existing authored rain is a minimum, with deterministic local weather fronts sampled by vehicle grip and rain visuals. Solo robbery rotation uses the accelerated clock (6-hour in-game slots); durable per-location UTC cooldowns remain independent and are enforced at reservation.

Every two in-game hours a seeded chance selects a nearby eligible road for a roadwork, disabled-vehicle or inspection event. Ambient spawns avoid that road and traffic prefers alternatives, with a reduced speed if no alternate exists. No hard physical roadblocks or global GPS closure are introduced: the player may still drive through, and events are cleared during active races, contracts and pursuit. This avoids stranding existing gameplay. A non-solid in-world diversion sign marks the affected road. These are advisory diversions, not fully simulated construction crews. Tests cover clock, schedule, weather and cooldown isolation. CI and real gameplay validation pending.
