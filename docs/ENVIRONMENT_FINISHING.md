# Phase 9 — Environmental finishing

World Settings → Environmental finishing (also Ctrl+K → Environmental finishing). Centre is captured from the camera. Choose residential, downtown, industrial, coastal or rural; set area half-width, spacing, density and seed. Preview the editable proposed props before Apply. Apply is a single undoable source transaction, respects the source revision, map capacity, terrain restrictions and conservative existing geometry/marker clearances. No new source schema or published world is introduced. Morrow uses its existing runtime-blocker-aware source view.

Runtime styling infers visual identity from existing gameplay-zone names, named source terrain parcels and named stock districts. Name a terrain parcel e.g. Industrial block, Downtown, Coastal park or Residential; the parcel's frontage within 32 m inherits its style. Explicit gameplay zones take precedence. Residential uses warm lanterns and greenery; downtown cooler lights and utility details; industrial amber lighting and pavement stains; coastal warm lamps and sand wear; rural sparse lights and shoulder wear. Road details are placed at global stations, exclude marked junction gaps, elevated roads, tracks and loose surfaces. The existing material and collision pipeline remains authoritative.

Night glows read the optional Phase 8 WorldDaylight CanvasModulate. No duplicate ambient clock is created. Streetlight positions are shared with the real procedural roadside furniture; authored lamps and roof-edge/entry glows use one bounded additive batch per physical streamed chunk, at most 72 sources, shared radial texture and material, lazy at night, zero extra physics or Light2D nodes. Daytime is the fallback without Phase 8; F9 toggles an isolated night preview in custom Test Drive when Phase 8 is absent. Map mirrors and editor overviews do not instantiate the lighting batch. Actual rendered appearance and GPU costs still require live verification.

CI includes deterministic planner, district palette, chunk ownership, station continuity, capacity and undo tests. Regional styling is visual-only; source scenery props may retain their existing gameplay collision properties. No hard road closures, new building interiors or per-window art are included.
