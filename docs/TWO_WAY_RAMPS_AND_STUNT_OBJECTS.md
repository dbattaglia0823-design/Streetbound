# Two-way ramps and placeable stunt ramps

Select On-ramp and set Lanes to 2 for a bidirectional entrance/exit (use 0 to -1 / -1 to 0 for tunnels). Two-way ramps have one lane each way and use ordinary junctions even if a former one-way merge mode is present. One-lane ramps retain directional highway merges.

In the native editor choose More tools → Place stunt ramp. Click to place on a clear ground-level area; Select/Edit to rotate, resize, move or delete. The orange lip indicates launch direction (local up at 0 degrees). No road or custom flight parameters are needed. The wedge's slope is derived from its geometry (rise = 15% of length). Crossing its uphill lip launches using actual incoming velocity, preserves lateral momentum and integrates gravity through touchdown. No preset speed window, runway or distance cap applies to new objects. Ordinary solid obstacles and map boundaries still constrain the 2D sweep. Visual body/wheels grow and lift as the independent grounded shadow spreads. Ground-level solo player jumps are supported during races/pursuits/contracts; networked jumps are disabled until multiplayer height synchronization is implemented. Legacy road jumps remain editable/removable with their existing constraints to preserve older maps and tests, but the legacy authoring control is hidden on roads without a jump.
